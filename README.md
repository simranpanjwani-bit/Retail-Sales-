Retail Sales Performance and Business Analysis Using PostgreSQL

1. Project Overview

The project, Retail Sales Performance and Business Analysis Using PostgreSQL, focuses on analysing retail sales data to understand business performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product sales and revenue trends.

Retail businesses generate large amounts of sales data every day. However, without proper data management and analysis, it becomes difficult to identify top-selling products, understand customer preferences, track revenue and make informed business decisions.

This project uses PostgreSQL to organise, manage and analyse retail sales data through SQL queries.

2. Problem Statement

Many retail businesses face challenges in managing and analysing their daily sales transactions. They often struggle to identify their best-performing products, understand customer purchasing patterns, monitor monthly sales trends and evaluate revenue generated by different product categories and stores.

Due to inefficient data management and limited analysis, businesses may find it difficult to make informed decisions regarding inventory management, product pricing and sales strategies.

Therefore, there is a need for a structured database system that can store retail sales information and generate meaningful insights to support better business decision-making.

3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- To develop a structured retail sales database using PostgreSQL.
- To organise customer, product, store and order information.
- To analyse product-wise and category-wise sales performance.
- To identify top-selling and low-performing products.
- To evaluate monthly and overall revenue trends.
- To analyse customer purchasing behaviour.
- To examine cancelled and returned orders.
- To generate meaningful business insights using SQL queries.
- To support data-driven business decisions.

5. Database Structure

The project uses five interconnected tables:

1. Customers

Stores customer information such as customer ID, customer name, city and customer segment.

2. Stores

Contains store information, including store ID, store name, city and state.

3. Products

Maintains product details such as product ID, product name, category, selling price and cost price.

4. Orders

Stores order-related information, including order ID, order date, customer ID, store ID, payment method and order status.

5. Order Items

Contains individual products associated with each order, including quantity, unit price and discount percentage.
